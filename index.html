GlassHome Launcher · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Applies to the app com.glasshome.launcher

In short

  • GlassHome has no account, no server of its own and no analytics. Your home screen layout, settings and usage stay on your phone.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Premium subscribers see no ads, and no ad requests are made for them.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • A few optional features (weather, news and online wallpapers) contact outside services only when you use them.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not collect your location, contacts, messages, photos or notifications.

This policy explains what information the GlassHome Launcher app (“GlassHome”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It covers the app only. Websites and services it links to have their own policies.

1. Information kept on your device

GlassHome is a home screen, so it has to remember how you set it up. All of the following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and none of it is sent to us:

  • Your layout and settings: pages, folders, groups, widgets, icon style, clock, gestures and appearance.
  • App launch counts: how often you open each app from GlassHome, used only to suggest apps in search. Counts fade automatically over time.
  • Wallpapers you choose: including a photo you pick through Android's own photo picker. GlassHome gets only the single image you select, not access to your gallery.
  • Depth effect analysis: the Depth feature analyses your wallpaper on the device to find its foreground. The image is never uploaded.
  • Weather city: the city you choose for the weather widget.
  • Premium status: a cached copy of whether you have an active subscription, so Premium keeps working when you are briefly offline.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google Account, Android may include GlassHome's settings in that backup so they return when you set up a new phone. This backup is stored by Google under your account, not by us. Wallpapers, photos and your Premium status are excluded from it.

2. Permissions and why they are used

GlassHome asks only for what a feature needs. Most sensitive permissions are requested only when you turn the related feature on, and you can revoke any of them in Android Settings.

PermissionWhat it is used for
InternetAds for free users, Google Play subscriptions, and the optional weather, news and online wallpaper features described below.
Calendar (read)Requested only when you add the Calendar widget. It reads the title, time and colour of upcoming events to display them. Events are not stored and never leave your device.
Notification accessOptional, for the Music widget only. Android requires this access for an app to show and control what is currently playing. GlassHome does not read, store or send the content of your notifications.
Device admin (screen lock)Optional. It lets the double-tap gesture or control centre turn the screen off. The only policy requested is “lock the screen”. GlassHome cannot wipe your device, see or change your password, or monitor you.
Modify system settingsOptional, for the control centre's brightness and rotation controls.
Do Not Disturb accessOptional, for the control centre's Do Not Disturb and silent-mode controls. It does not give access to notification content.
Set wallpaperApplies the wallpaper you pick to your lock screen and system wallpaper.
Request uninstallLets the “Uninstall” menu item open Android's own uninstall confirmation. GlassHome cannot remove apps by itself.
Installed appsA launcher has to list your apps to show them. GlassHome sees only apps that appear in your app drawer, plus icon packs, and does not request the broader “all packages” permission.
Motion sensorsThe Depth effect's tilt uses the phone's motion sensor, processed on the device. No permission is needed and nothing is recorded.

GlassHome does not request access to your location, contacts, phone calls, SMS, camera, microphone or files.

3. Services GlassHome connects to

Google AdMob (advertising, free version only)

The free version shows ads in the app library, the Today page, search, and briefly after you apply a wallpaper. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalised. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, GlassHome shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and no ads are requested until you have answered it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › About GlassHome › Ad privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings under Privacy › Ads (on some phones Google › Ads). If you subscribe to Premium, GlassHome makes no ad requests.

Google Play Billing (subscriptions)

GlassHome Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment, and we never receive your card or bank details. The app receives only the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, which it uses to unlock Premium. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Open-Meteo (weather widget, optional)

When you use the weather widget, GlassHome sends the city name you search for and the coordinates of the city you picked to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. It does not use your phone's GPS, and the request carries no identifier for you or your device.

News feeds (optional, off by default)

News is off until you turn it on. When it is on, GlassHome downloads only the feeds you choose (by default, Google News). Each feed's publisher receives a standard web request from your phone, including your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. Feeds are fetched over encrypted HTTPS connections only.

NexWall (online wallpapers, optional)

The “Discover” section of the wallpaper picker browses and downloads wallpapers from the NexWall service. When you use it, your requests, including any search terms, go to NexWall, which receives a standard web request from your phone, including your IP address. Nothing is sent to NexWall unless you open Discover.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not operate servers that receive your data, and we have no user accounts.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We do not read the content of your notifications, messages or files.

5. Children

GlassHome is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains ads, so it is intended for a general audience of users aged 13 and over.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data stays on your phone, you control it directly:

  • Delete everything GlassHome stores: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings › Apps › GlassHome › Storage.
  • Android backup: manage or delete it from your Google Account settings.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 3, or use Google's My Ad Center.
  • Revoke permissions: at any time in Android Settings › Apps › GlassHome › Permissions, and from the special-access screens for notification access and device admin.

We keep no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you would like confirmation of this, or have a request under data protection law (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU/UK GDPR), contact us by email or phone using the details below. Data held by Google, Open-Meteo, news publishers or NexWall is covered by their own policies and request processes.

7. Security

Data stored by GlassHome lives in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Network requests made by GlassHome use encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Changes to this policy

If GlassHome's handling of information changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
